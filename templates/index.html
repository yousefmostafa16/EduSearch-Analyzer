About EduSearch Analyzer

Learn about our platform and technology

A comprehensive platform for discovering and analyzing online educational courses using advanced data analytics and network analysis techniques.

What We Do

Data Collection

We scrape and collect course data from major educational platforms including YouTube and Coursera.

Analytics

Our platform processes this data to provide insights through visualizations, statistical analysis, and trend identification.

Network Analysis

Using graph theory and network analysis, we identify relationships between topics, instructors, and courses.

Smart Search

Our intelligent search system allows you to filter and discover courses based on multiple criteria.
